# Banoffee Pie Recipe
## Ingredients
* 1 1/2 cups digestive biscuits, crushed
* 1/4 cup granulated sugar
* 1/2 cup unsalted butter, melted
* 2 ripe bananas, sliced
* 1 cup heavy cream
* 1/2 cup confectioners' sugar
* 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
* 1 cup caramel sauce
* 1 cup whipped cream
* Chocolate shavings or chopped nuts for garnish
## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350°F (180°C).
2. In a bowl, mix crushed biscuits and sugar.
3. Add melted butter and mix until well combined.
4. Press mixture into a 9-inch tart pan with a removable bottom.
5. Bake for 15-20 minutes or until lightly browned.
6. Arrange sliced bananas on top of the crust.
7. In a separate bowl, whip heavy cream until stiff peaks form.
8. Add confectioners' sugar and vanilla extract, and mix well.
9. Spread whipped cream over the bananas.
10. Drizzle caramel sauce over the whipped cream.
11. Top with chocolate shavings or chopped nuts.
12. Refrigerate for at least 2 hours before serving.
